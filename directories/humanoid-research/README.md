## 🚀 Start here

1. Open the original project and locate its paper / citation and evaluation setup.
2. Compare embodiment, task, simulator and evaluation assumptions rather than just headline metrics.
3. Keep original citations with any research notes or reproduced results.

Read the [engineering guide](GUIDE.md) for a practical selection checklist. [resources.json](resources.json) contains the structured entries.
